# QR Platform: business user guide

This guide is for business owners and managers using the dashboard.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open the platform and choose **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number). Add your phone number if you also want the code by SMS.
3. Enter the 6-digit code we send you. It expires after 10 minutes. Choose **Send a new code** if it doesn't arrive.
4. Set up your business: name, type, phone, location and brand colour. You can change all of this later in **Business settings**.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email, then the code and a new password.

## 2. Create a QR code

1. Go to **QR codes → New QR code**.
2. **Type**: choose what the code opens:
   - **Website**: any web page.
   - **Digital menu**: one of your menus (create the menu first).
   - **Social profile**: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and others. For WhatsApp, enter the number to open a chat.
   - **Contact card**: saves your details to the customer's phone.
   - **Location**: opens directions in Google Maps.
   - **Document**: a PDF you upload, or a link to one.
   - **Plain text**: a short message.
3. **Content**: give the code a name only you will see (for example "Table 4" or "October flyer"), fill in the details, and optionally set an expiry date.
4. **Design**: pick colours, add your logo in the centre, and set the quiet zone and damage tolerance. The preview updates as you go. If the colours are too faint for phones to read, we'll tell you before you save.
5. Choose **Create QR code**, then download it as **PNG** (posters, social media) or **SVG** (large prints, signage).

## 3. Change where a code goes without reprinting

Every code is dynamic. Open the code and edit **Destination**. Printed copies go to the new place on the next scan. **Destination history** shows every change.

Need to pause a code? Choose **Deactivate**. Scans then show a "not active right now" page until you choose **Activate**. A code past its expiry date shows an "expired" page.

You can delete a code only if it has never been scanned, because a scanned code may already be in print.

## 4. Digital menus

1. Go to **Menus → New menu**, give it a name (for example "Lunch").
2. Add sections (Starters, Mains, Drinks), then add items with a price, description and photo.
3. Use the switch on an item to mark it **sold out** for the day. Customers still see it, greyed out.
4. Turn on **Published** so customers can open it.
5. Choose **Create QR for this menu** to make a code for your tables or counter.

## 4b. Event pages (weddings, funerals and more)

1. Go to **Events → New event** and pick the occasion: wedding, funeral or celebration of life, birthday, church service, conference, or something else.
2. Choose a template (Ivory & Gold, Kente, Garden Blush, Black & Gold, Kobene Red and others), type the names and the date, then select **Create event**. You get a starter programme for that occasion and the event's own QR code.
3. **Content** tab: edit the names, date, time, venue, welcome note and photos. Edit, add, reorder or remove sections: programme line-up, text (for example a biography), hymns or songs, people (bridal party, chief mourners, speakers) and photos.
4. **Design** tab: switch template at any time without losing your words, or change the colours, fonts and decoration yourself. The phone preview updates as you type.
5. **QR card** tab: choose Poster (A4/A5), Table card (4 × 6 in) or Square (for WhatsApp), edit the wording ("Welcome to the wedding of…", "Scan for the programme line-up"), then **Download PNG** or **Print or PDF**.
6. Select **Publish**. Until then, guests who scan see a "not ready yet" page, so you can print the card early and finish the programme later. Changes after printing show on the next scan.

## 4c. Getting help

Choose **Help** at the bottom right of any dashboard page and ask in your own words, for example "How do I print my funeral programme card?". The assistant knows this guide, the screen you're on and your plan, and links you to the right page.

## 5. Scan statistics

Open any QR code to see scans over time, unique visitors, and the device, operating system, browser, country and city behind each scan. Location is approximate and comes from the network, never from GPS. We never store visitors' IP addresses.

The **Overview** page shows the last 14 days across all your codes and your most-scanned codes this week.

## 6. Account and business settings

Use the profile button at the top right for **Business settings** and **Sign out**. Settings hold your business details, logo, brand colour, currency and tax rate (tax applies once ordering and invoicing go live).

## 7. Plans and payment

New businesses get a free trial of every Pro feature. After the trial you're on the **Free** plan (3 codes, 1 menu, 500 scans a month) unless you choose a plan.

When you reach a limit or try a paid feature (your logo in a code, SVG downloads, country and city stats), an **Upgrade to continue** box explains what's needed. Choose **See plans**, or open **Plan & billing** from the sidebar or your profile menu.

On **Plan & billing**:

1. See your current plan, when it ends and what you've used this month.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then **Choose Basic** or **Choose Pro**.
3. Pay on the secure payment page with mobile money (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) or card. For mobile money, approve the prompt on your phone.
4. You come back to a confirmation page. Your plan is active straight away.

Each payment covers one month or one year. We show a reminder a week before it ends. **Renew** early and the extra time is added on. If a plan ends, your printed codes keep working, but some editing may be locked until you renew. Only the business owner can make payments.
